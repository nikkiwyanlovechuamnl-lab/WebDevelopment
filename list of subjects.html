    1. Lesson 2 Erik Erikson's Psychosocial Development Theory

    1. Erik Erikson's Psychosocial Development Theory is a theory that states that development occurs in eight stages, each characterized by a specific psychosocial conflict that must be resolved for healthy development. According to this theory, individuals face different challenges and tasks at each stage of life, and their ability to successfully navigate these challenges influences their overall well-being and social functioning.
    2. My understanding of Erik Erikson's Psychococial Development Theory is that it explains how individuals develop through eight distinct stages, each with its own unique challenges and opportunities for growth.

    1. Lesson 3 Daniel Goleman's Emotional Intelligence Theory

    1. Daniel Goleman's Emotional Intelligence Theory is a theory that states that emotional intelligence is a critical factor in personal and professional success. It emphasizes the importance of understanding and managing one's own emotions, as well as recognizing and empathizing with the emotions of others. According to this theory, individuals with high emotional intelligence are better equipped to navigate social interactions, build relationships, and make informed decisions.
