How to make short bread

Ingredients needed:

  • Butter
  • Sugar
  • All purpose flour
  • Additional Topings of your choice(not required)

  1. Prepare

Gather all required ingredients needed, and the require tools.

Tools needed

  • Mixing bowl
  • Spatula
  • Measuring cups
  • Baking pan/tray
  • Parchment paper

For the ingredients there must be atleast 1½ sticks of butter, 250g of sugar, and 200g of all purpose flour.

  1. Mix

Now put the ingredients in the mixing bowl with around ⅓ cup of sugar, 1 cup & 2 tbsp of butter and lastly 1 cup of all purpose flour. Here we have our ingredients in the bowl we can now mix them together until they are well combined, and forms a dough.

  1. Preparing the pan

Here we prepare the baking pan by lining it with parchment paper, once you have measured the parchment paper add some butter onto the pan and spread it around. Once done add the parchment paper on top of the buttered pan, here we add a little more butter on the parchment paper to prevent the dough from stinking after it bakes.

  1. Shape & Pre-heat

Now that we have made the dough, our next step is to shape it. while we shape the dough we can pre-heat the oven to 325°F (162°C). Now that the oven is pre-heated, we can shape the dough into any shape we want, here we can add any toppings we want as we finish shaping the dough.

  1. Bake

Once we are done adding topings and shaping the dough, we can place it onto the pan/tray, once done we can now finally place the pan/tray into the oven to bake for 15-20 minutes, or till its golden brown.

  1. Cool, Decorate & Enjoy

Finally we can take the pan/tray out of the oven, and let it cool for around 10 minutes, once cooled you can decorate the short bread how ever you wish, and after that you can now enjoy your short bread.
